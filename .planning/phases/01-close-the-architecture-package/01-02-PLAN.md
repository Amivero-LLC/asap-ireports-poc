quietly omits what it did not build is the exact failure ADR-001 is written against. The distinction
between "coming in Phase 3" and "deliberately not coming" must be readable off the page, never
inferred.

discussed, settled, and then cut with the dual-adapter bake-off (ARCH-05). ORCH-01 builds the port

1. Load the case — a `CaseManifest` initializes a `RunManifest`.
2. Determine the criteria to analyze, and fan out one specialist sub-call per criterion.
3. Inside one specialist sub-call: a case-filtered, bounded-K vector-plus-lexical query through the
   retrieval port against the indexed synthetic case; the retrieved spans become the evidence the
